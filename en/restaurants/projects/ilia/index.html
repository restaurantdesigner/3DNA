The project

A restaurant designed for lingering

TVIT is a restaurant in development, designed around one simple idea: every table should feel like the best seat in the room. Tall windows, framed by textured plaster pilasters and wrapped in warm light, fill the dining room with daylight and turn it into a soft glow as evening falls.

Sculptural pendant lamps in woven natural fibre hang at different heights, drawing a living ceiling over the marble tables. Curved-back wooden chairs, a long banquette beneath the windows, sofa corners with natural-wood tables and a bar with backlit shelving create a journey made for long dinners, meetings and one last drink.

Greenery comes into the room as a material in its own right: it adds freshness, separates areas without closing the views and guides guests from the entrance to their table.

Light
Windows framed by warm indirect light and woven lamps that bring the ceiling down to the scale of each table.
Materials
Textured plaster, marble, wood and natural fibres: a warm, tactile and calm palette.
Spaces
Dining room, window banquette, lounge and bar, joined in one continuous journey.

3D design by 3DNA

From kitchen to dining room, all designed in 3D

At 3DNA we design TVIT as a single journey: the dining room, the bar and the professional kitchen are planned together in one 3D model, so the atmosphere guests experience and the work that happens behind it run as one restaurant.

The video shows the project the way we work on it: from line drawings to a space with materials and light. The kitchen is organised by workstation, with steel surfaces, hoods and equipment placed so the team moves freely, while the dining room keeps its warmth with natural-fibre lamps, wood and a long banquette.

Before building starts, the owner can walk through their restaurant, decide from real images and avoid mistakes that later cost time and money.

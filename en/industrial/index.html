Interactive tools

Three ways to explore an industrial plant

From a detailed digital twin of a real-scale facility to a one-slider view of production scale — each tool runs in the browser, no installation needed.

  • Detailed digital twin

    Interactive Factory

    What it is

    A detailed, interactive 3D digital twin of a 14 million-litre-per-year Canadian ethanol production facility, inspired by public information.

    What it shows

    • Every piece of equipment with its own data sheet
    • The production process and nine engineering networks — power, steam, cooling, water, air, wastewater, control and fire
    • An investment dashboard: CAPEX, EBITDA, margin, payback and scenarios
    • Realistic, X-ray, thermal, blueprint and night views
    Explore the factory →

  • Parametric factory generator

    FactoryBuilder

    What it is

    A parametric generator: enter a production target and the tool designs a complete conceptual ethanol plant in 3D, with its equipment, layout and investment.

    What it shows

    • Equipment sized and counted for every production stage, with standby units
    • A generated 3D layout with pipe rack, roads and engineering connections
    • Capacity analysis: configured capacity, bottleneck, footprint and checks
    • Indicative CAPEX, OPEX, EBITDA and payback; three scenarios side by side; CSV equipment list
    Build your own factory →

  • Production scale at a glance

    Plant Configurator

    What it is

    The simplest way to understand production scale: one question, one slider and one clean 3D workspace — no engineering knowledge needed.

    What it shows

    • How many silos, fermenters, columns and tanks a plant needs, from 5 to 100 million litres per year
    • Each unit as a separate object, added or removed as you move the slider
    • The number of units above every production zone
    • Two capacities side by side in compare mode
    Open the configurator →

All configurations, layouts and figures are conceptual and illustrative — not certified engineering.

03 Process visualisation

From grain to product, step by step

In the Canadian ethanol case study, the process runs through twelve areas, from wheat receiving to 99.5 % ethanol, wet distillers grain for the adjacent feedlot and the utilities that support them.

  1. 01Grain receiving and storage
  2. 02Milling
  3. 03Cooking and liquefaction
  4. 04Fermentation
  5. 05Distillation
  6. 06Dehydration (molecular sieve)
  7. 07Storage and dispatch
  8. 08Co-products: WDG and thin stillage
  9. 09Utilities: steam, water and air
  10. 10Wastewater treatment
  11. 11Electrical and fire protection
  12. 12Administration and control

04 Equipment exploration

Every piece of equipment, with its function and connections

Select a fermenter, a column or a tank and the digital twin highlights it, brings the camera closer and shows its role in the process, its specifications and the equipment it connects to.

05 Investment and costs

Investment scenarios in real time

The calculator links the facility to its economics in Canadian dollars: production, yield, wheat, ethanol and distillers-grain prices, natural gas, electricity, personnel and unit investment. Every change updates CAPEX, OPEX, EBITDA, margin, cost per litre and payback.

06 Who it is for

One tool for every party in the project

  • Industrial investors

    Understand the plant, the process and the project's economics before committing the investment, and compare scenarios visually.

  • Equipment manufacturers

    Present your machinery inside a complete plant, in context and with its connections, for trade fairs, offers and sales training.

  • Engineering companies

    Communicate the layout and the process to clients and financiers with an interactive model, clearer than drawings and static presentations.

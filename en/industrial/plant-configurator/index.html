What the Plant Configurator shows

Choose an annual ethanol output and the plant is rebuilt in front of you. Each grain silo, fermenter, distillation column, molecular-sieve unit, storage tank and utility unit appears as a separate object, at one consistent physical scale, grouped in production zones that follow the process: grain storage, milling, mash preparation, fermentation, distillation, dehydration, co-products, ethanol storage and utilities.

The quantities come from the 3DNA FactoryBuilder configuration engine, which converts the annual target into hourly and daily flows and sizes every production stage. Click any unit to see its capacity, the required quantity and its utilization, or switch on Compare to place two capacities side by side.

Generated configurations are conceptual engineering estimates for visualization — they require independent professional engineering validation.

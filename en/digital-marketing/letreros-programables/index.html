02 The company

Made in Spain, a catalogue for every business

Letreros Programables manufactures and distributes programmable LED signs and illuminated signs for businesses. Manufacturing is in Granada and distribution reaches all of Spain and the rest of Europe.

  • 6product families
  • P4–P10indoor and outdoor screens
  • USB · WiFiprogramming from a computer or phone
  • Onlineshop with card, PayPal and bank transfer

Where we see the opportunity

  1. 01A wide catalogue presented today as a list: customers need to see each product in context (pharmacy, bar, shop window).
  2. 02Key specifications (size, pitch, faces, programming) are not visible in the listing, so every product has to be opened.
  3. 03The website is only in Spanish, although distribution reaches the rest of Europe.
  4. 04Calls to action are limited to calling or getting in touch; there is no direct path to a quote or WhatsApp from each product.
  5. 05Instagram points to a hashtag rather than a company profile: the product is highly visual and video is not being used.

03 Website redesign

From catalogue to a shop window that sells

A new, independent website built on Shopify that shows the product switched on, in context and with the information customers need to decide. It is not a modification of the current PrestaShop shop.

What changes

  • Clear navigationFive entries: Products, Industries, How it works, Company and Contact.
  • Catalogue by family and industryCustomers start from their product or their business: pharmacy, bar, hotel, shop, car park…
  • Specifications in viewSize, pitch, faces and programming on every card, without opening the product page.
  • ConversionQuote and WhatsApp buttons always visible, on every product too.
  • TrustManufacturing in Granada, free shipping, payment methods and setup help, up front.
  • Mobile firstThe design starts from the phone, where visits from Instagram and Facebook arrive.

04 Multilingual website

Spanish, English and German

The company already distributes in Europe. Presenting the catalogue in the languages of those markets opens up searches that don't reach the website today.

Concept design · the same page in the three languages

  • International presentation

    A distributor or a business in Germany, Austria or Switzerland understands the product and asks for a quote in its own language.

  • Multilingual SEO

    Each language with its own URLs, titles and descriptions, and the language versions linked for Google.

  • Agreed scope

    Premium includes the agreed content in English and German. Essential and Business are delivered in Spanish; more languages or content can be added in phases.

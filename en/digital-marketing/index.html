03 What digital marketing is for

The goal first. Then the channels.

Posting on social media, buying ads or redesigning the website is not a strategy. Those are tools. A strategy starts when you decide what has to change in the business: more people who know you, more bookings, more sales, customers who come back or a stronger reputation.

Each goal calls for different channels, content and metrics. A campaign that announces an opening is not measured like a lead-generation campaign or a loyalty programme. So we work the other way round: we define the goal, choose the channels that serve it and measure only what shows whether it has been achieved.

04 The goals

All the purposes of digital marketing

A business rarely needs all of them at once. Choosing two or three and doing them well achieves more than spreading effort across every one.

  1. 01

    Brand awareness

    Make sure your audience knows you exist and remembers you when they need what you offer.

    Strategies

    • Performance advertising
    • Social media and influencers
    • Launch and pre-opening
    • Brand and storytelling

    Channels

    • Social media
    • Video
    • Display advertising
    • Press and partnerships

    How it is measured

    Reach, impressions and searches for your brand.

  2. 02

    Positioning and differentiation

    Make the market associate you with something specific and set you apart from competitors.

    Strategies

    • Brand and storytelling
    • Reputation management

    Channels

    • Brand identity
    • Storytelling
    • Original content
    • Space design

    How it is measured

    Brand perception, mentions and the price customers accept.

  3. 03

    Qualified traffic

    Bring people with real interest to your website, not empty visits.

    Strategies

    • Inbound marketing
    • SEO and content

    Channels

    • SEO
    • Google Ads
    • Content marketing

    How it is measured

    Organic visits, engagement and pages per visit.

  4. 04

    Local visibility

    Show up when someone nearby searches for what you offer.

    Strategies

    • SEO and content
    • Local marketing

    Channels

    • Local SEO
    • Google Business Profile
    • Google Maps
    • Directories

    How it is measured

    Maps views, calls and direction requests.

  5. 05

    Lead generation

    Turn visits into contacts: forms, bookings, calls or messages.

    Strategies

    • Inbound marketing
    • Performance advertising
    • Sales funnel

    Channels

    • Landing pages
    • Forms
    • Lead magnets
    • Lead ads

    How it is measured

    Number of leads, cost per lead and conversion rate.

  6. 06

    Sales and conversion

    Guide the customer all the way to the purchase, booking or signature.

    Strategies

    • Performance advertising
    • Sales funnel
    • Data and optimisation

    Channels

    • Sales funnels
    • Retargeting
    • Email
    • Conversion optimisation

    How it is measured

    Sales, cost per acquisition and return on ad spend.

  7. 07

    Higher average spend

    Help each customer buy more or better: bundles, extras and cross-offers.

    Strategies

    • Sales funnel
    • Email and automation
    • Loyalty and CRM

    Channels

    • Email
    • Website and digital menu
    • Automation

    How it is measured

    Average spend and revenue per customer.

  8. 08

    Loyalty and repeat business

    Make customers who already bought come back and choose you again.

    Strategies

    • Email and automation
    • Loyalty and CRM

    Channels

    • Email
    • Loyalty programmes
    • CRM
    • WhatsApp

    How it is measured

    Repeat rate and customer lifetime value.

  9. 09

    Win back inactive customers

    Bring back people who stopped coming with a concrete reason to return.

    Strategies

    • Performance advertising
    • Email and automation
    • Loyalty and CRM

    Channels

    • Email
    • WhatsApp and SMS
    • Retargeting

    How it is measured

    Reactivated customers and recovered revenue.

  10. 10

    Community and engagement

    Build a relationship with your audience beyond the sale.

    Strategies

    • Social media and influencers

    Channels

    • Social media
    • Customer content
    • Events

    How it is measured

    Comments, shares and active followers.

  11. 11

    Online reputation

    Look after what people say about you: reviews, ratings and mentions.

    Strategies

    • Local marketing
    • Reputation management

    Channels

    • Google Business Profile
    • Review platforms
    • Replying to reviews
    • Social listening

    How it is measured

    Average rating, number of reviews and mentions.

  12. 12

    Launches and openings

    Build anticipation before a venue opens or a service launches.

    Strategies

    • Performance advertising
    • Social media and influencers
    • Launch and pre-opening

    Channels

    • 3D renders and videos
    • Waiting lists
    • Advertising
    • Local press

    How it is measured

    Pre-registrations, opening bookings and reach.

  13. 13

    Educate and inform

    Explain what you offer and answer questions before the purchase.

    Strategies

    • Inbound marketing
    • SEO and content

    Channels

    • Blog and guides
    • Video
    • FAQs
    • Newsletter

    How it is measured

    Reading time, downloads and qualified enquiries.

  14. 14

    Understand the customer

    Data to decide with: who buys, why and through which channel.

    Strategies

    • Loyalty and CRM
    • Data and optimisation

    Channels

    • Web analytics
    • Surveys
    • A/B tests
    • CRM

    How it is measured

    Segments, test results and the source of each sale.

  15. 15

    Employer brand

    Attract and keep the people who will make the business work.

    Strategies

    • Social media and influencers
    • Brand and storytelling

    Channels

    • LinkedIn
    • Social media
    • Careers page

    How it is measured

    Applications received and candidate quality.

05 The strategies

Digital marketing strategies

A strategy is how a goal is achieved: what is done, in what order and through which channels. These are the main ones and the goals each one serves.

  1. 01

    Inbound marketing

    Attract customers with useful content instead of interrupting them with ads.

    Key actions

    • Content that answers customers' real questions
    • Downloadable resources in exchange for contact details
    • Automatic follow-up of every lead

    Goals

    • Qualified traffic
    • Lead generation
    • Educate and inform
  2. 02

    SEO and content

    Appear on Google when customers search for what you offer.

    Key actions

    • Research into what your customers search for
    • One clear page for each service and search
    • Articles and guides published regularly

    Goals

    • Qualified traffic
    • Local visibility
    • Educate and inform
  3. 03

    Performance advertising

    Paid campaigns measured by what they achieve: leads, bookings or sales.

    Key actions

    • Google Ads for people already searching
    • Social ads for a defined audience
    • Retargeting people who have shown interest

    Goals

    • Brand awareness
    • Lead generation
    • Sales and conversion
    • Win back inactive customers
    • Launches and openings
  4. 04

    Sales funnel

    A journey designed from the first contact to the purchase.

    Key actions

    • One message for each stage: discover, compare, decide
    • A landing page with a single action
    • Follow-up by email or WhatsApp

    Goals

    • Lead generation
    • Sales and conversion
    • Higher average spend
  5. 05

    Local marketing

    Be the visible choice for people searching nearby.

    Key actions

    • A complete, active Google Business Profile
    • Reviews requested and answered
    • Campaigns limited to your area

    Goals

    • Local visibility
    • Online reputation
  6. 06

    Social media and influencers

    Build a brand and a community where your audience spends its time.

    Key actions

    • A content calendar by format
    • Partnerships with local or industry creators
    • Quick replies to comments and messages

    Goals

    • Brand awareness
    • Community and engagement
    • Launches and openings
    • Employer brand
  7. 07

    Email and automation

    Personal messages that go out on their own at the right moment.

    Key actions

    • A welcome sequence
    • Messages after a purchase or visit
    • Reminders that win back inactive customers

    Goals

    • Higher average spend
    • Loyalty and repeat business
    • Win back inactive customers
  8. 08

    Loyalty and CRM

    Know each customer and give them reasons to come back.

    Key actions

    • A segmented customer database
    • A loyalty or rewards programme
    • Offers based on purchase history

    Goals

    • Higher average spend
    • Loyalty and repeat business
    • Win back inactive customers
    • Understand the customer
  9. 09

    Launch and pre-opening

    Reach opening day with an audience and anticipation.

    Key actions

    • 3D renders and videos before opening
    • A waiting list or early bookings
    • An opening event or campaign

    Goals

    • Brand awareness
    • Launches and openings
  10. 10

    Brand and storytelling

    A story and an identity that make your business recognisable.

    Key actions

    • A defined value proposition and tone
    • A consistent visual identity on every channel
    • Real stories about the team and the space

    Goals

    • Brand awareness
    • Positioning and differentiation
    • Employer brand
  11. 11

    Reputation management

    Protect and improve what people say about your business.

    Key actions

    • Ask for a review after every good experience
    • Reply to every review
    • Track mentions of the brand

    Goals

    • Positioning and differentiation
    • Online reputation
  12. 12

    Data and optimisation

    Decide with data and improve what already works.

    Key actions

    • Measurement of every channel and every sale
    • A/B tests of pages and ads
    • Regular reports that lead to decisions

    Goals

    • Sales and conversion
    • Understand the customer

06 The channels

The tools of a digital strategy

You don't need to be everywhere. Each channel is chosen for the goal it serves and for where your customer is.

  1. 01SEO Organic visibility on Google for the searches your customers make.
  2. 02Local SEO Google Business Profile, Maps and reviews for people searching nearby.
  3. 03Search advertising Google ads for people already looking for what you offer.
  4. 04Social media advertising Meta, TikTok or LinkedIn to reach a defined audience.
  5. 05Social media Organic content that builds the brand and a community.
  6. 06Content marketing Articles, guides and videos that attract people and answer questions.
  7. 07Email and automation Messages that arrive at the right moment, without manual work.
  8. 08WhatsApp and messaging Service and follow-up on the channel customers already use.
  9. 09Websites, landing pages and funnels Pages designed to turn a visit into a customer.
  10. 10Retargeting Reach again the people who have already shown interest.
  11. 11Partnerships and influencers Reach new audiences through voices they already trust.
  12. 12Visibility in AI assistants Clear, structured information so you appear in AI answers.
  13. 133D visual content Renders, videos and walkthroughs that show the space before it exists.
  14. 14Analytics and measurement Data to know what works and where to invest.

07 The space as content

Your venue communicates too

At 3DNA, space design and marketing start together. The renders, videos and 3D walkthroughs we create for a project exist before the venue opens, and they become material for the website, social media, ads and the opening campaign.

So when the business opens its doors, it already has an audience, content and a consistent image across every channel.

The project is designed once. The content works every day.

08 How we work

How we build a strategy

  1. 01Diagnosis

    We analyse the business, the customer, the competition and what already works.

  2. 02Goals and metrics

    We choose the priority goals and how each one will be measured.

  3. 03Channels and budget

    We select the channels that serve those goals and allocate the investment.

  4. 04Content and launch

    We create the messages, visuals and pages, and launch the actions.

  5. 05Measure and improve

    We review the data regularly and adjust whatever is not delivering.

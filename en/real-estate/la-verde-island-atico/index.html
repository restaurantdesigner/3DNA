Description

Two or three-bedroom penthouses with two bathrooms (one en-suite) on the top floor of La Verde Island. A terrace plus a rooftop solarium reached by an external staircase, pre-installed for a jacuzzi and an outdoor kitchen.

The three-bedroom penthouse has an L-shaped kitchen with island; the two-bedroom one a U-shaped kitchen with peninsula. Parking, a storage room and access to the club with spa, pools, gym and padel are included.

About the development: La Verde Island

Expected completion
Q2–Q3 2029

La Verde Island is a new residential development in Doña Julia, between Estepona and Sotogrande, on one of the best plots in the area: frontline golf, with panoramic golf and sea views.

It offers 2 and 3-bedroom apartments and penthouses in a gated community with 24/7 security, a clubhouse, spa, pools, gym, padel courts and a golf practice area for residents. Every home includes a parking space and a storage room.

Images: renderings for guidance only. Furniture and decoration not included. Decorative wall panels are not offered; wood kitchen finishes and LED strips are optional. Penthouse solariums include pre-installation for a jacuzzi and outdoor kitchen only. Views depend on the position of each home.

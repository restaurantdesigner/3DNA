Description

Two-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment at Sunway Residence, a new-build gated community in Estepona. The living room and kitchen form one open space leading to the terrace, with floor-to-ceiling windows.

The master bedroom has an en-suite bathroom. The home comes with a fitted kitchen, a parking space and a storage room. Ground-floor homes have a private garden. There are 16 apartments of this type in the development.

About the development: Sunway Residence

Developer
Investhome Development
Architecture
Villarroel architecture studio
Homes
48

Sunway Residence is a new-build development in Estepona, on the Costa del Sol, designed by the Villarroel architecture studio. Its 48 homes are set in four blocks around a garden with a pool, inside a gated community with automatic access.

The homes are designed around natural light and outdoor living: floor-to-ceiling windows, generous terraces, private gardens on the ground floor and solariums on the penthouses.
